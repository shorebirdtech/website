In a few short years Shorebird has become a trusted partner for Flutter
developers worldwide. Helping them to deliver mobile updates over-the-air
without the friction of lengthy app store review cycles. Along the way, many of
you have shared your expertise and knowledge with the wider Flutter community
writing blogs, giving conference talks, answering questions in Discord, and
helping your peers ship better Flutter apps. This work has had a direct impact
in helping developers discover and adopt Shorebird.

## Introducing the Community Ambassador program

We want to recognize these developers with the creation of the Shorebird
Community Ambassador program, and today we’re opening up applications. This
program celebrates community members who share their experience with Shorebird
through blog posts, tutorials, conference talks, workshops, videos, podcasts or
in any other format that fits their style. We want ambassadors to create content
and advocate for Shorebird in their voice, not ours. Authenticity is the whole
point.

Beyond content creation, Community Ambassadors serve as technical experts who
support other developers, answer questions in the Discord, and create a
welcoming environment for everyone learning Shorebird and Flutter.

Ambassadors will focus on one or more of the following:

- Creating technical content
- Speaking at meetups, conferences, and community events
- Organizing or supporting local Flutter and Shorebird gatherings
- Mentoring developers who are new to over-the-air updates or Flutter
- Sharing feedback from their region with the Shorebird team

Being a community ambassador comes with several perks and benefits including
conference support, discount codes, early access to features and roadmap, and
swag.

## Who we’re looking for

We’re looking for Flutter developers with hands-on Shorebird experience and
genuine enthusiasm to help others succeed. You don’t need to be a senior
engineer or have massive social followings. What matters is a passion to teach
and share your experiences.

Shorebird is used by developers around the world and this program is open to all
applicants. That said, our largest user bases are in India, Brazil, and
Indonesia with Japan and Vietnam growing rapidly. We’re especially eager to hear
from developers in these regions.

If you’re interested in becoming a Shorebird Community
Ambassador[fill out this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfHeULk8JMGIdRX9Xfs2W_5SXH3sMP43HW7PSKMNhs89tDnuA/viewform).
Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis.
