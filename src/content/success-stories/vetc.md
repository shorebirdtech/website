VETC, a subsidiary of Tasco, is an electronic toll collection service in
Vietnam. The organization provides a comprehensive suite of digital services
such as payment processing, transportation logistics, and customer platforms.
Their products serve a large user base and require frequent updates with high
reliability. Given the scale of their operations and user base, VETC has to
offer uncompromising system reliability. For a system that facilitates daily
commutes and financial transactions, any disruption can lead to user
dissatisfaction and operational bottlenecks.

## The challenge

The VETC mobile apps are built in Flutter and allow users to recharge, view
transactions, and check vehicle usage history. The app store release cycle has
posed a significant barrier to agility and has limited developer productivity.
When critical fixes are necessary the team would need to create an emergency app
store submission and then wait for approval. Waiting for third-party approvals
became an operational risk.

## The Shorebird solution

Recognizing the need for a more resilient deployment strategy the VETC team
chose Shorebird to deliver over-the-air (OTA) updates eliminating the
traditional bottlenecks of app store review cycles. Code Push transformed their
release process giving them the ability to patch bugs, update application logic
and make small improvements without going through the full app store release
process.

“Shorebird gave us a “hotfix pipeline” to address urgent fixes for our Flutter
apps, reducing hotfix release times from several days to a few hours,” said Trần
Hán Hiếu.

With Shorebird VETC has been able to deliver hotfixes immediately reducing the
impact to users during incidents. By drastically reducing the administrative
overhead associated with app store submissions VETC has optimized its
engineering resources. Developers are now positioned to iterate faster and
conduct safe testing in production environments.
